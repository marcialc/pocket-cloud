# Self-hosted fonts

Latin subset woff2 files, downloaded from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com), both SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Loaded by the `@font-face` rules in `src/client/styles.css` and preloaded in `index.html`.
Listed on `public/licenses.html`; keep it in step with this file.

- `inter-latin.woff2`: Inter v20, variable weight (covers 400/500/600/700). License: `OFL-Inter.txt`.
- `press-start-2p-latin.woff2`: Press Start 2P v16. License: `OFL-PressStart2P.txt`.
